## SLIDE 1: How to Play Virgil Feud

### **The Setup**
- **Two teams** compete to win points by guessing survey answers
- **One host** controls the game via keyboard (no clicking, just pure game show magic)
- **First to 500 points wins** (or highest score after 3 rounds)

### **The Flow**
1. **Face-Off** → Teams decide who controls the board for this question
2. **Guess Answers** → Host reveals correct guesses by pressing `1-5` keys
3. **3 Strikes & You're Out** → Wrong answers = strikes. After 3 strikes, the other team can steal
4. **Bank Points** → After all answers revealed or 3 strikes, host banks points to the controlling team
5. **Steal Opportunity** → If a team strikes out, the other team gets ONE chance to steal all points
6. **Next Question** → Rinse and repeat through 3 rounds

### **Scoring**
- Each answer has a point value (based on survey popularity)
- Reveal all answers or play until 3 strikes
- Controlling team banks points (or loses them to a steal)
- First to 500 points = **CONFETTI TIME** 🎊

### **The Vibe**
Shout out answers. Celebrate reveals. Roast bad guesses. Trash talk encouraged. Let's go.

## SLIDE 2: Host Controls (AKA The Cheat Sheet)

### **Your Keyboard is the Game Console**

#### **Core Controls** (You'll Use These Constantly)
- **`1` `2` `3` `4` `5`** → Reveal answers in order
- **`X`** → Add a strike (3 strikes = team switch)
- **`Spacebar`** → Bank points to the active team
- **`S`** → Steal! Award points to the other team
- **`Enter`** → Move to next question/round
- **`ESC`** → Show/hide this control overlay

#### **The Host Flow**
1. Teams face off → You decide who guesses first (manual, use your judgment)
2. Team calls out answers → Press `1-5` to reveal matches
3. Wrong answer? → Hit `X` to add a strike
4. All answers revealed OR 3 strikes? → Press `Spacebar` to bank points
5. Team struck out? → Other team gets a steal attempt → Press `S` if they get it right
6. Press `Enter` → Next question begins

#### **Pro Tips**
- **No mouse needed.** Your hands never leave the keyboard.
- **Press `ESC` anytime** to see controls overlay during gameplay.
- **Don't stress.** The game tracks everything (strikes, scores, round progression).
- **Have fun.** You're the ringmaster. Own it.
